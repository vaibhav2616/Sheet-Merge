How Alternation Works
Option 1 (In-Sheet Apps Script):
Share this Google Sheet with your secondary Google account (`Editor` permission). Open the sheet on Account 2 and set the Daily Trigger at a different hour (e.g. Account 1 at 09:00 AM, Account 2 at 03:00 PM). Both accounts update the single `SheetMerge Status` column sequentially!

Option 2 (FastAPI Backend):
Authenticate multiple Gmail API credentials with the local FastAPI server (`http://localhost:8000/auth/add-account`). The server alternates dispatches across configured accounts automatically.
